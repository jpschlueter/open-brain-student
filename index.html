🎙️ Voice capture

Tap the microphone and speak. Tap again to stop. Review the text, fix anything it misheard, then save. Works best in Chrome or Edge.

Tap to start

📹 YouTube

Paste a video link and click Capture automatically. Your server fetches the transcript and saves it. If it can't (no transcript, or the free monthly quota is used up), it tells you and shows a box to paste the transcript yourself.

  1. Click Get Transcript below. The video opens on YouTube in a new tab.
  2. Under the video, click ...more in the description, then scroll down and click Show transcript.
  3. The transcript appears on the right side. Click inside it, select all of it (click the first line, scroll to the end, Shift + click the last line), and copy.
  4. Come back here and paste it into the box below.

📄 PDF

Drop a PDF and its text is pulled out right here in your browser. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to pull out.

Drag a PDF here, or click to choose a file

🔗 Web article

Paste a link and click Fetch automatically. Your server reads the page and saves its text. Some pages block readers or need a login; for those, paste the text yourself below.

📥 Bulk import

Paste many links at once, one per line. YouTube links get their transcript; everything else is fetched as an article. A line starting with # names the collection for the links below it (for example # EMS Research). Other lines are ignored. Links already in your brain are skipped.

🔍 Search your brain

Finds every thought that contains your words (keyword search). In Level 6 you will add search by meaning.
